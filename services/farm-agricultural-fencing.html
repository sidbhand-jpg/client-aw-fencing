FAQ

Farm & Agricultural Fencing Questions

What is the best fence for livestock containment?

The best fence depends on the type of livestock. Woven wire is excellent for smaller animals, while high-tensile and barbed wire are effective for larger animals like cattle.

Can you assess fencing for larger acreage?

Yes. Share the property address, approximate acreage, livestock type, and required gates so the team can assess the layout and confirm the scope.

Do you offer repair services for farm fences?

Absolutely. We provide repair services to maintain the integrity and functionality of your existing farm and agricultural fences.
